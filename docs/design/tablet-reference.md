## Tablet Visual Direction

- Build from the mobile flow first, then use tablet space for stronger review, comparison, and editing surfaces.
- Preserve the dark graphite base, restrained copper and champagne accents, compact operational density, and large touch targets.
- Prefer split-panel layouts where they reduce navigation churn: case overview plus next actions, evidence list plus document detail, checklist plus requirement detail, packet preview plus export history.
- Keep primary actions visible without hover and avoid desktop-only interactions at this breakpoint.
- Use the tablet references to validate spacing, panel width, bottom navigation behavior, and readable line lengths at the `768 x 1024` QA viewport.
